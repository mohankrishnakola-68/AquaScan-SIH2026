## 📌 PART 1: COMPLETE CONCEPT MASTER SUMMARY

Use this section to memorize the core concepts so your team speaks with confidence on camera.

### 1. The National Challenge (The "Why")
* India has been granted exploration rights over **75,000 square kilometers in the Central Indian Ocean Basin (CIOB)** by the **International Seabed Authority (ISA)**.
* Under the Government of India's **₹4,077-Crore Deep Ocean Mission (Samudrayaan)**, scientists must locate critical battery metals:
  * **Polymetallic Nodules**: Nickel (Ni), Copper (Cu), Cobalt (Co), and Manganese (Mn) for EV batteries.
  * **Hydrothermal Massive Sulphides (SMS)**: Seafloor vent deposits rich in Copper, Zinc, Gold, and Silver.
  * **Cobalt-Rich Crusts**: Strategic Rare Earth Elements (REEs).
* **Current Bottleneck**: Deep-sea ROVs and research vessels (*ORV Sagar Nidhi*) cost **₹35–₹50 Lakhs per day** to operate. Traditional Ocean Bottom Seismometers/Nodes (OBS) cost over **₹50 Lakhs each**. Consequently, dense seabed grid surveys are economically impossible.

### 2. The AquaScan Solution (The "What")
* **AquaScan-OBS** is a low-cost, autonomous, deployable **"Drop-and-Pop" Seafloor Metal Detection Lander**.
* A research vessel drops 20 to 50 pods into the ocean while continuing its transit.
* Each pod free-falls to the seabed, logs metallic nodule density and hydrothermal sulphide redox anomalies, releases its iron ballast plate via an **electrolytic galvanic burn-wire**, floats back to the surface, and transmits GPS coordinates via LoRa to the ship for recovery.

### 3. The 3 Technical Pillars (The "How")
1. **Transient Electromagnetics (TEM / Pulse Induction)**: 
   * Seawater is an electrical conductor ($\sigma \approx 4.5\ \text{S/m}$), which blinds normal metal detectors.
   * AquaScan pulses a magnetic field and uses **delayed time-gating ($>35\ \mu\text{s}$)**. Seawater eddy currents dissipate instantly, leaving only the long decay response of metallic nodules.
2. **Self-Potential (SP) Redox Sensing**:
   * Non-polarizable $\text{Ag/AgCl}$ electrodes passively detect natural battery voltages ($-20\text{ to }-250\ \text{mV}$) generated by hydrothermal massive sulfide deposits without draining battery power.
3. **Electrolytic Burn-Wire Ballast Release**:
   * Eliminates ₹25-Lakh acoustic release transponders with a ₹250 sacrificial wire that electrolytically dissolves in seawater under low DC current in under 3 minutes, allowing positive buoyancy ascent.
4. **Disruptive Cost**:
   * **Target unit cost < ₹40,000 INR (~$460 USD)** — over 95% cheaper than commercial ocean landers, enabling wide-area swarm deployments.
