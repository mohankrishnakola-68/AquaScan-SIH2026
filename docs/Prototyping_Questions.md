# Prototyping & SIH Questions

This document tracks the core questions and context for the AquaScan project related to SIH 2026.

## Theme & Problem Selection
**Question:** Compare robotics themes and their chances in SIH selection. Could you compare the proposed robotics ideas like Smart Khadi Assistant Robot and Disaster Rescue Robot in terms of difficulty, innovation, impact, and SIH final round chances? Which one would you recommend for a CSE/IoT team aiming for scalability and government impact?
**Answer / Selection:** 
The chosen project is **AquaScan – Low-Cost Deployable Seafloor Metal Detection Sensor for Ocean Resource Exploration (PS ID: 26064)** under the **Disaster Management** theme. This theme was selected because the AUV helps detect underwater metallic hazards, locate submerged debris, support search & recovery, and improve coastal safety, making it a stronger fit than Clean & Green Technology or Smart Vehicles.

## Deep-Sea Simulation
**Question:** What are effective methods and accessible tools or software to simulate deep-sea pressure, corrosion, and communication challenges for underwater sensor projects? How detailed should these simulations be to satisfy SIH judges, and what documentation is advisable?
**Context:** Real deep-sea tests are challenging; scaling designs with prototypes tested in shallow water is acceptable for SIH.

## Prototype Development
**Question:** Can you suggest detailed steps and affordable components for building a shallow-water prototype of the seafloor metal detection sensor, considering limited resources and time? Also, what are common pitfalls to avoid during prototype development?
**Focus:** 
- Building a scalable shallow-water prototype.
- Focusing on Seafloor Metal Detection rather than Software-Defined Sonar for feasibility and easier demonstration.
